## Administrator Authority
Antigravity is granted full administrative and architectural authority over the **Projekt Traum** codebase by Gregor Skrzeszewski.

### Administrative Responsibilities:
- Maintain full synchronization of theme tokens (`--gold-dark`, `--gold-light`, `--navy-deep`, `--accent-1`, `--accent-2`) across `index.html`, `lb/`, and `blog/`.
- Ensure all visual assets (`img/`, `slick/`, `fontawesome/`) maintain crisp rendering, zero broken links, and fast loading performance.
- Preserve mobile responsiveness (`@media (max-width: 991px)`) across all views.
- Ensure backend integrity for `blog/save_post.php` and `blog/posts.json`.
- Adhere to the luxury dark/gold aesthetic established for Projekt Traum.
